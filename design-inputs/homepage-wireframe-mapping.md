# Homepage Wireframe Mapping — Intent Studios

> **Source of Truth Hierarchy:**
> 1. Content & section sequence: `intent-studios-final-sitemap.html` (13 homepage sections).
> 2. Brand identity, palette & voice: `Intent_Studios_Brand_Foundation.pdf` & `Intent_Studios_Visual_Brain.pdf` + updated Inter-first typography direction.

## Authoritative Homepage Sequence (from `intent-studios-final-sitemap.html`)

1. **Header** — Logo lockup (`Intent` + `STUDIOS`), Work, Services, About, Contact, and primary button (`Book a 15-minute discovery call`).
2. **Hero** — Niche headline, one primary button (`Book a 15-minute discovery call`), secondary action (`See Our Work`).
3. **Proof bar** — Three to five verifiable studio proof points with context (clearly structured placeholders where real numbers await confirmation).
4. **The problem (in buyer quotes) + what we do and don't** — Authentic buyer frustrations from `Intent_Studios_Brand_Foundation.pdf` paired with Intent Studios' clear positioning (not a cheap solution, not a shortcut factory, not order-takers).
5. **Related work** — One featured project + two smaller projects.
6. **Client quote (video first)** — Video-first testimonial slot with real-video-ready frame, name, role, company, credibility line.
7. **Service cards, goal chips & industry line** — The 4 official services (`Website Design & Build`, `Product & App Design`, `Brand Identity`, `Content & Social`), interactive goal chips, and live industry line.
8. **How We Work + Fit / Not a Fit lists** — 5-stage studio process (`Discovery`, `Strategy`, `Design`, `Execution`, `Launch`) paired with explicit `Best fit` and `Not a fit` qualification lists.
9. **Founder block** — Short founder note + portrait slot (real photography placeholder — never AI-generated face) + Ansh Kapila handles.
10. **Intent Circle teaser** — Concise introduction + link to `/intent-circle/`.
11. **FAQ** — Homepage-specific answers to key client objections.
12. **Closing band** — High-conviction closing line + `"Book a 15-minute discovery call"` + sign-off `"Intent is in the Details"`.
13. **Footer** — Multi-column directory (Studio, Services, Who we help, Intent Circle, Talk to us, Privacy & Terms) + monumental bottom brand wordmark.
